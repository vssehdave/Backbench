# 11.2 Carry, equity tranche - index hedging

- Section: 11.2
- Asset class: Structured assets
- Harness status: **not testable: bonds/rates** - needs credit tranche, CDS or MBS data.

## Description (paper)

This strategy amounts to buying the equity (lowest quality) tranche and Deltahedging it by selling the index. The Delta (i.e., the hedge ratio) is given by 178 ∆ *ix = D ix (487)

The premiums received from the equity tranche are higher than the premiums paid on the short index position. The risk is the exposure to equity tranche credit events.
